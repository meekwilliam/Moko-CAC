TEXT: A candlestick is just a picture of price over a fixed time period — its <b>open</b>, <b>close</b>, <b>high</b>, and <b>low</b>. Learning to read them is chart literacy, not a trading signal.
CHART: uptrend

[TF] A candlestick only shows the price at one single moment in time.
ANSWER: False
WHY: A candlestick summarizes an entire time period (like 5 minutes), not a single instant.

[FILL] The thick middle part of a candlestick is called the [___].

WHY: The body spans between the open and close prices.

[MATCH] Match the candlestick part to what it shows.
Body => The range between the open and close price
Wick => The high or low price reached during that period, even if it didn't close there
Color => Whether the close was higher (bullish) or lower (bearish) than the open
WHY: Each part of a candle encodes a different piece of price information.

[FILL2] A [1] candle typically means the close was higher than the open, while a [2] candle means the close was lower.
BLANK1: *green | red | grey
BLANK2: *red | green | grey

Hammer | detail: A specific single-wick shape | wrong: A hammer typically has one long wick, not two.

[SPOT] Someone says "this candle proves price will definitely go up next."

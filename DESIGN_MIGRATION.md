# Moko Visual Identity Migration

## Product direction

Moko is a premium interactive market training game. The market is the main character: charts, liquidity, executions, progression, and decisions should dominate the visual hierarchy.

The redesign must preserve navigation, `GameData.json`, decoding, answer validation, question state, missed-question requeueing, XP, streaks, lives, test-out and jump-ahead rules, lesson resuming, completion, and every question mechanic.

## Current-system audit

1. **Design system**
   `LessonTheme.swift` owns the central palette, font mapping, answer surfaces, chart cards, press styles, and feedback effects. `ThemeManager.swift` supplies the single dark palette and full-screen background. Several screens still use local literals and one-off surfaces.

2. **Typography**
   Sora is bundled in Regular through ExtraBold and already serves headings, chrome, and important numbers. Plus Jakarta Sans Regular and SemiBold already serve body, question, and answer text. This matches the new direction and should be retained. Technical market data currently uses Sora; it should move to a dedicated monospaced token.

3. **Colors**
   The app is dark navy/slate, but it mixes bright rainbow unit colors, brand green, orange streaks, red hearts, a green gem currency, and a blue-pink-amber premium gradient. Green and red are also used correctly for buyer/bullish and seller/bearish market data. The new system must reserve those two hues for market meaning.

4. **Spacing**
   Spacing is generally mobile-readable but informal, with repeated values from 2 to 34 points. The migration will standardize a 4-point scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, and 40.

5. **Reusable components**
   Shared primitives include `Chunky3DStyle`, `SinkButtonStyle`, `Circle3DStyle`, `RowPressStyle`, `OptionSurface`, `AnswerFeedbackFX`, `ChartCard`, `QuestionPrompt`, `OFOption`, and `OFMultiOption`. These are the safest high-leverage migration points.

6. **Navigation**
   `MainTabView` is a persistent six-tab shell. Its custom dog house, target, trophy, heart, and person illustrations are colorful and cartoon-like. Routing itself is clean and must remain unchanged.

7. **Lesson progression**
   `PathView` uses a sinusoidal winding path, large 3D pucks, breathing halos, crown nodes, floating speech-tail test-out labels, popover cards, and rainbow unit colors. This is the single strongest Duolingo cue. `PathStore` owns the actual progression state and can be reused behind a new linear skill-map presentation.

8. **Lesson question UI**
   `LessonView` provides a close button, progress bar, lives, horizontal question paging, a scrolling mechanic body, and a fixed action/feedback footer. The pager and state separation are functional strengths. The glossy capsule progress bar and large raised controls are visual migration targets.

9. **Answer buttons**
   Generic and order-flow answers share thick rounded borders, a dark lower slab, a sinking press, correct-answer pop, and wrong-answer shake. Validation is separate from presentation, so the visual behavior can be safely replaced.

10. **Success and failure states**
    `FeedbackBanner` uses sparkles or a broken heart, full green/red tinting, `Correct` or `Oops`, an XP callout, a `WHY?` button, and a springing bottom sheet. It should become a restrained decision debrief that keeps exact explanations and correct-answer logic.

11. **Lives**
    Hearts are stored and regenerated by `ProgressStore`; the UI is in `HeartBadge`, `HeartsView`, the path HUD, and lesson header. The logic remains. The visual metaphor will become focus or session capacity rather than glossy cartoon hearts.

12. **Streaks**
    Streak dates and counts are sound. The current flame, spin, pop, and orange celebration closely mirror language-learning apps. The new presentation should use training continuity, calendar data, and a premium achievement object.

13. **XP**
    XP is global and awarded by `ProgressStore`. It appears in the HUD, feedback, profile, test-out results, and reward screen. It should be framed as mastery progress and remain numerically unchanged.

14. **Achievements**
    `ProfileStore` already receives badge data, but `ProfileView` does not render the badges. This is an opportunity for original 3D mastery objects without changing backend data.

15. **Test out**
    Assessment generation and competency scoring are handled outside the visual layer and must remain. The path entry point, lesson badge, and result treatment can become a technical placement challenge rather than a speech-bubble shortcut.

16. **Boss battles**
    Boss identity comes from lesson metadata, final-unit structure, crown icons, and reward copy. The underlying lessons are valuable synthesis assessments. Their visual presentation should become high-stakes market drills using the simulation board and a distinct violet expert state.

17. **Trade-decision simulations**
    `OrderFlowSimulationView` is the most on-brand mechanic. It already combines price, DOM, tape, footprint, delta, CVD, and volume profile. It should set the visual standard for the rest of the app, not be rebuilt.

18. **Question mechanics**
    Active course content currently uses multiple choice, true/false, matching, sequence building, tap candle, mark level, draw trendline, aggressor, book drag, book comparison, DOM tap, evidence selection, replay comparison, Time & Sales, footprint, footprint tap, CVD tap, volume-profile tap, live chart, trade decision, and trade scenario. The router also supports fill blanks, multi-fill, ingredient match, tap multiple candles, consolidation marking, OHLC labeling, number sliders, trade management, entry selection, invalidation marking, and composite simulation taps/selections. All routing and grading remains intact.

## Strongest Duolingo cues to remove

1. Winding lesson path and isolated 3D pucks.
2. Thick slab shadows and exaggerated sink/pop physics on nearly every button.
3. Cartoon navigation illustrations and mascot-based app logo.
4. Glossy hearts, flame streak celebration, gem currency, crowns, and bone-oriented test copy.
5. Speech-tail test-out prompt and floating lesson bubbles.
6. Rainbow unit palette and green used as a non-market brand color.
7. Sparkles, broken-heart failure art, `Oops`, and overshooting feedback sheets.

- Mastery teal: `#25C7B7`
- Progress blue: `#4B86FF`
- Expert violet: `#8B6CFF`
- Premium magenta: `#D75BCB`
- Buyer/bullish only: `#32C98A`
- Seller/bearish only: `#F26472`
- Warning: `#E8B45B`

Cards use 14 to 20 point corners, one-pixel borders, a faint top inner highlight, minimal shadow, and no offset slab. Motion uses short ease-out or critically damped transitions, subtle scale, opacity, slide, glow, and data-driven updates.

## Migration phases

Implementation status: all five phases have now been applied to the active app shell. The retired `PathView` remains in source as a non-rendered compatibility fallback; `SkillMapView` is the active progression UI.

### Phase 1: Foundation

Status: complete.

- Add semantic brand, market, typography, spacing, radius, surface, and motion tokens.
- Replace chunky shared button and option styling with low-depth technical controls.
- Add a monospaced market-data font token.
- Replace flat page fills with a deep navy layered market backdrop.
- Keep compatibility wrappers so mechanics do not need structural rewrites.

### Phase 2: Navigation and progression

Status: complete.

- Replace cartoon bottom navigation art with a restrained monochrome market icon system and labels.
- Replace the winding puck path with a linear skill map made of compact lesson modules, unit progress, state rails, and synthesis checkpoints.
- Replace rainbow units with tier-based teal, blue, violet, and magenta accents.
- Restyle test-out as a placement challenge while preserving all availability and scoring rules.

### Phase 3: Lesson and game-state shell

Status: complete.

- Restyle the lesson header, progress, question cards, action area, debrief, exit dialog, completion, test-out results, and boss presentation.
- Replace hearts visually with focus/session capacity while preserving the same count, spend, refill, unlimited, and gating logic.
- Replace flame streak celebration with training continuity and calendar-based achievement visuals.
- Render existing profile badges as premium mastery objects.

### Phase 4: Mechanics and simulation polish

Status: complete for shared surfaces, technical typography, selection states, and market-semantic color usage. Existing authored mechanic layouts and grading remain unchanged.

- Apply technical typography and shared market surfaces to DOM, tape, footprint, CVD, volume profile, and composite simulations.
- Keep focused teaching visuals focused; reserve the full multi-signal board for trade decisions.
- Improve visual density, selected states, data-driven motion, and expert-state hierarchy without changing targets or validation.

### Phase 5: Brand assets and QA

Status: complete. The new market mark is installed in the launch screen, active shell, and iOS app-icon appearances. The simulator target builds and launches successfully.

- Replace the mascot app logo and icon with an original market-data mark.
- Verify onboarding, home, lesson, wrong/right feedback, completion, streak, lives, profile, test-out, boss, and trade-decision flows.
- Validate `GameData.json`, build the iOS simulator target, and regression-check every routed mechanic.
